**Hard lines enforced in this SOP:**

- **The delivery driver NEVER counsels, takes new prescription orders by phone, or decides substitutions.** If a patient asks a clinical question, the driver says, "Let me get the pharmacist for you," and contacts the pharmacy.

## Purpose

Deliver filled prescriptions to patients safely, privately, and on time — with an unbroken chain of custody from the pharmacy to the patient's hands, proof of delivery for every delivery, and undelivered medication back in the pharmacy the same day.

## Scope

All deliveries by the pharmacy's own driver and by any contracted courier: scheduling, preparation, handoff, transport (including cold-chain where applicable), proof of delivery, failed deliveries, returns, and controlled-substance deliveries (restricted — see step 9). Covers the delivery radius, fees, and recordkeeping. Does not cover dispensing (SOP-02) or counselling (SOP-07).

## Owner role (who is accountable)

- **Owner (accountable):** sets the delivery radius, the delivery fee or free-delivery threshold (with the CPA), and whether a driver is employed or a courier is contracted. Reviews delivery cost per delivery monthly.
- **PIC (responsible):** owns the delivery procedure, decides which prescriptions may be delivered, and sets and approves the controlled-substance delivery rule with counsel.
- **Pharmacist/RPhT:** prepare, seal, label, and log out deliveries; confirm the prescription was verified before it leaves.
- **Driver/courier:** follows the route, protects privacy and temperature, obtains proof of delivery, and returns undelivered medication the same day. Never counsels, never takes orders, never decides substitutions.

## Prerequisites

1. The delivery radius, fee/threshold, and driver-vs-courier model are set in writing by the owner with the CPA.
2. Delivery bags/containers, cold-chain packaging where applicable, and proof-of-delivery forms (or system capture) are available.
3. The driver/courier is trained on this SOP, privacy (HIPAA), and the "fetch the pharmacist" rule, and has signed the training record.
4. Commercial auto / non-owned-auto insurance is confirmed with the broker where delivery is offered (master report: confirm coverages and limits; no premium stated).
5. The controlled-substance delivery rule is set by the PIC and approved by counsel before the first controlled-substance delivery — many independents restrict or prohibit it. Do not deliver a controlled substance until that rule exists in writing.

## Numbered steps

1. **Schedule the delivery.** Front-store staff schedule the delivery (patient request, address, phone, promised time window). Confirm the address and a contact number. Record the delivery in the delivery log with the prescription number(s).
2. **Prepare the delivery.** The pharmacist/RPhT seals, labels, and logs out each prescription. Confirm the prescription was finally verified (SOP-02) before it leaves the pharmacy. Attach the delivery label with patient name and address visible only as needed for delivery — protect other patient information.
3. **Two-identifier check at handoff to the driver.** The person handing over the delivery and the driver both confirm the patient name and prescription number(s) against the delivery log. Both sign/initial the log-out.
4. **Transport.** Keep deliveries sealed, upright, and out of public view. Do not leave medication unattended in a vehicle. For refrigerated products, use the validated cold-chain packaging and record the departure time; on arrival, confirm the cold-chain condition per the PIC's instruction. **[CONFIRM WITH COUNSEL/CPA]:** confirm the exact cold-chain transport and documentation standard for this pharmacy with the PIC/counsel before first use — the master report requires a monitored refrigerator and excursion quarantine in-store but does not state a transport temperature standard.
5. **Deliver to the named patient (or their authorised representative) only.** Check two identifiers at the door. Do not leave a prescription with a neighbour, at a doorstep, or with an unidentified person unless the PIC's written procedure expressly permits it for that patient — and record the authorisation.
6. **Obtain proof of delivery.** The recipient signs (or the system captures compliant proof of delivery) for every delivery. Record the time, the recipient's name, and the signature. The claim is billed only when delivered with proof of receipt (SOP-04).
7. **Offer the pharmacist.** If the patient asks any clinical question, the driver contacts the pharmacy and the pharmacist speaks to the patient. The driver never answers it.
8. **Return undelivered medication the same day.** Any prescription that cannot be delivered returns to the pharmacy the same day, is logged back into will-call, and the delivery log is updated. Never keep undelivered medication overnight in a vehicle or at a driver's home.
9. **Controlled substances.** Deliver only under the PIC/counsel-approved rule (prerequisite 5). If no rule exists, do not deliver controlled substances. If the rule permits delivery, follow its identification, signature, and logging requirements exactly, and report any failed controlled-substance delivery to the PIC the same day.
10. **End-of-day delivery reconciliation.** The PIC or designated staff reconciles the delivery log: every logged-out delivery is either signed as delivered or logged back into will-call. Investigate any delivery that is neither, the same day.

## Controls (what prevents error)

- Two-identifier check at pharmacy handoff and at the patient's door.
- Sealed, labelled deliveries; proof of delivery for every delivery.
- Same-day return of undelivered medication; end-of-day reconciliation of every logged-out delivery.
- Driver never counsels, takes orders, or decides substitutions.
- Controlled-substance delivery only under a written PIC/counsel-approved rule.
- Cold-chain packaging and excursion quarantine for refrigerated products.

## Records produced (what is filed and where)

| Record | Filed where | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery log (prescription number, patient, address, driver, log-out time, proof of delivery, return time if undelivered) | Delivery file / PMS | Per counsel instruction |
| Proof-of-delivery signatures (or system capture) | With the delivery log | Per PBM contract + counsel instruction |
| Cold-chain departure/arrival notes (where applicable) | With the delivery log | Per counsel instruction |
| Failed-delivery and return-to-will-call entries | Delivery log + will-call record | Per counsel instruction |
| Monthly delivery cost-per-delivery and retention report | Owner's monthly pack (CPA) | Per CPA instruction |

## Failure modes (what goes wrong and the response)

| Failure | Response |
|---|---|
| Patient not home / no authorised recipient | Do not leave the medication unless the PIC's written procedure permits it for that patient. Return it the same day, log the failed delivery, and call the patient to re-schedule. |
| Proof of delivery missing | Treat the delivery as unproven. Contact the patient to confirm receipt, log the outcome, and notify the PIC. Do not bill the claim as delivered without proof (SOP-04). |
| Delivery is lost, damaged, or tampered with | Notify the PIC immediately, log the incident (SOP-09), and follow the PIC's instruction on replacement and any reporting. Do not re-dispense without pharmacist verification. |
| Cold-chain breach in transit | Quarantine the product on return, log the excursion, notify the PIC. Do not dispense the affected product until the PIC clears it. |
| Driver asked a clinical question by the patient | Driver contacts the pharmacy; the pharmacist speaks to the patient. Log the contact. Retrain the driver if they answered it themselves. |
| Controlled-substance delivery fails or is refused | Return it the same day, notify the PIC the same day, and log it. Do not attempt a second delivery without the PIC's instruction. |
| Delivery log does not reconcile at end of day | Investigate the same day: check will-call, the vehicle, and the driver log. Escalate any unaccounted prescription to the PIC immediately — treat as a potential loss (SOP-09/SOP-10 if controlled). |
