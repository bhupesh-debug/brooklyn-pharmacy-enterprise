## Purpose

Handle every dollar that enters the pharmacy — cash, card, and copay — so that the day's takings reconcile to the day's sales, discrepancies are found the same day, and no single person can take, lose, or mis-record money undetected.

## Scope

Daily cash handling: opening float, drawer counts, cash drops, safe counts, deposits, card reconciliation, refunds/voids, petty cash, and discrepancy investigation. Covers the POS, the cash drawer, and the safe. Bank reconciliation itself is the bookkeeper/CPA's task and is checked against this SOP's daily close pack.

## Owner role (who is accountable)

- **Owner (accountable):** sets the float, the over/short investigation threshold (with the CPA), the deposit schedule, and the refund authorisation rule. Reviews the daily close pack and the weekly over/short log. The owner may perform the second-person check but never checks their own close.
- **PIC:** checks the daily close when acting as second person and ensures prescription-sale cash reconciles to adjudicated claims.
- **Cashier(s):** one cash drawer per cashier per shift; count, record, and drop cash as directed; never share a drawer.
- **Bookkeeper:** reconciles the bank to the daily close packs, reviews the POS refund report weekly, and counts petty cash weekly.

## Prerequisites

1. POS system is live and tied to the PMS sales record.
2. The opening float amount, the over/short threshold, and the deposit schedule are set in writing by the owner with the CPA. The master report's example threshold ($10) is an **assumption** — set the actual threshold with the CPA before opening; do not adopt $10 by default.
3. A safe is installed; safe access is limited to named persons (SOP-09).
4. Daily close forms are available at the POS.

## Numbered steps

1. **Opening float.** At opening, the cashier counts the float against the float record, signs the drawer count, and reports any variance to the PIC/owner before the first sale (SOP-01). Do not start trading on an unverified float.
2. **One drawer per cashier per shift.** Each cashier uses only their own drawer. Never share a drawer, and never leave cash in an unlocked drawer.
3. **Collect the copay as adjudicated, every time** (SOP-04). Record the prescription number on the POS transaction where the system requires it. A waived or discounted copay needs counsel-cleared policy — do not improvise one at the till.
4. **Cash drops during the day.** Drop excess cash to the safe during the day on the schedule set by the owner. Record each drop (amount, time, cashier, witness where required).
5. **Card payments.** Process cards only through the approved processor. Reconcile card totals to the processor's settlement at the daily close. Report any failed or duplicated card transaction to the bookkeeper the same day.
6. **Daily close (every trading day, no exceptions).** The cashier: (a) reconciles the POS total to the PMS sales and to the physical cash in the drawer; (b) reconciles credit/debit-card totals to the processor's settlement; (c) reconciles the copay/cash collected per prescription to the adjudicated claims for the day. Record all three reconciliations on the daily close sheet.
7. **Second-person check.** The close is performed by one person and checked by a second (PIC or owner/bookkeeper), and both sign. The checker never checks their own close.
8. **Over/short.** Any over/short above the threshold set with the CPA is investigated the same day and logged. Investigate the cause (miscount, wrong change, missed transaction) before the next trading day. Do not "make it up" from the next day's takings.
9. **Safe count.** The safe is counted by two people. Record the count, both signatures, and any variance.
10. **Deposits.** Deposit cash on the fixed schedule (daily or next business day, as set by the owner/CPA). Match the deposit slip to the daily close. The person who handles cash must not be the person who reconciles the bank.
11. **Refunds and voids.** Only a pharmacist or the owner may authorise a refund/void. Record the reason, the prescription number, and the authoriser. The owner/bookkeeper reviews the POS refund report weekly.
12. **Petty cash.** Keep a fixed float, a written log, and receipts for every item. The bookkeeper counts petty cash weekly. No IOUs — ever.

## Controls (what prevents error)

- Segregation of duties: cash handler ≠ bank reconciler; orderer ≠ sole receiver; dispenser ≠ self-verifier; poster of a payment ≠ approver of it.
- Two-person daily close and two-person safe count, both signed.
- One drawer per cashier per shift; no unlocked cash; scheduled cash drops.
- Copay collected as adjudicated, every time.
- Refunds/voids authorised only by a pharmacist or the owner, with a recorded reason.
- Fixed petty-cash float with receipts and a weekly count; no IOUs.

## Records produced (what is filed and where)

| Record | Filed where | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Opening float count (signed) | Daily close pack | Per CPA instruction |
| Cash-drop log | Daily close pack | Per CPA instruction |
| Daily close sheet (POS vs PMS vs cash; card settlement; copay vs adjudicated claims; both signatures) | Daily close pack, filed by date | Per CPA + counsel instruction |
| Over/short log (amount, cause, action) | Daily close pack / weekly summary to owner | Per CPA instruction |
| Deposit slips matched to daily closes | Finance file | Per CPA instruction |
| POS refund/void report (weekly review) | Finance file | Per CPA instruction |
| Petty-cash log and weekly count | Finance file | Per CPA instruction |

## Failure modes (what goes wrong and the response)

| Failure | Response |
|---|---|
| Drawer is over/short above the threshold | Investigate the same day, log it, and notify the owner/PIC. Recount with a second person before concluding the amount. Do not adjust the next day's float to hide it. |
| Deposit does not match the daily close | Hold the deposit record open, reconcile the close sheet line by line with the bookkeeper, and log the resolution. Do not post a balancing entry without the CPA's instruction. |
| Card settlement does not match card totals | Report to the bookkeeper the same day; check for failed/duplicated transactions and processor timing differences before treating it as a loss. |
| Refund/void without authorisation | Flag it in the weekly refund-report review. The owner/PIC investigates and retrains; repeated unauthorised refunds trigger SOP-11 escalation. |
| Cash found outside the drawer or safe | Log it immediately, notify the owner/PIC, and count it with a second person. Treat as an incident (SOP-09) until the source is established. |
| Safe count variance | Two-person recount immediately. If unresolved, notify the owner/PIC, log it as an incident, and review access records (SOP-09). |
